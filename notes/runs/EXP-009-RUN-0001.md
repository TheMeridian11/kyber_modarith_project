## Interpretation and security scope

Both cumulative variants improve every one of their 108 operation/block comparisons with the original in this session. EXP-003's median reductions span 5.97–19.94%; EXP-005's span 10.72–19.59%. These ranges span different operation/parameter groups and are not an aggregate workload score or evidence that one variant universally wins. These session-1 data support substantial cumulative benefit; the complete report separately assesses repeatability across all three sessions.

EXP-005's additional keygen reductions are +5.26%, +0.49% and +1.62% for Kyber512/768/1024. The Kyber768 comparison contains one negative block. Kyber1024 encapsulation is effectively flat at −0.01236%, with six positive and six negative pairs; decapsulation is −0.35645%, with all twelve pairs slower. Other unchanged-operation movements are also retained. These changes do not establish a specific compiler/layout mechanism. The [complete comparison](../final_comparison.md) assesses these tradeoffs across all three sessions; this record alone supplies no cross-session conclusion.

EXP-003 remains central to the thesis: EXP-005 already includes its inverse-NTT change and the earlier optimisations. This direct cumulative comparison does not isolate EXP-003's incremental contribution; its earlier EXP-002 comparison answers that question. I neither add old incremental percentages nor infer a new security conclusion from faster or less variable performance.

Exact source and normal-code matches support scoped reuse of the accepted compatibility, invalid-input, range/scale/narrowing, UBSan, dynamic-flow and statistical-screen findings. I did not rerun those dedicated validation/security experiments in EXP-009. The run adds successful benchmark round trips and verifies build identities; it adds no timing-leakage observations. EXP-007 directly tested original/EXP-005/EXP-006, and EXP-008 directly tested original/EXP-003/EXP-005. Whole-KEM constant time, tiny-leak sensitivity, physical/fault channels and other platforms remain unestablished. The inherited general-dot local-canary tradeoff remains recorded for both candidates.
