## Conditions and limitations

I measured normal, uninstrumented GCC 13.3.0 builds with the existing -O3 flags, five adjacent pairs per parameter, 100 warm-up triplets and 1000 measured exchanges per process. EXP-005 runs first in odd blocks. Every block uses the same deterministic seed-1 fixtures. CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW reports nanoseconds with 1 ns resolution; empty probes have 11–12 ns minima. I subtract no timer overhead and remove no outliers. The full duration range, including 15861–1334125 ns observations, is retained.

The recorded environment is Ubuntu 24.04.2 under WSL2, kernel 5.15.167.4, on an Intel Core i9-12900KS with 24 guest-visible logical CPUs. Affinity is guest logical CPU 0. Host/power/background conditions and physical-core placement remain unknown where not recorded. The matched process pair is the experimental unit for the incremental claim; 90000 inner durations are not 90000 independent replications. This is one session on one build/target. I cannot add or multiply separate-run incremental medians into an original-baseline gain.

## Assembly, correctness and security findings

The [RUN-0001 review](EXP-006-RUN-0001.md) remains the correctness/arithmetic gate. It includes exact 100-fixture normal/UBSan transcript comparisons, sampled invalid/noncanonical inputs, an independent ordinary-int64 target oracle, explicit range/scale/narrowing arguments and upstream functional tests. The timing binaries match the reviewed bodies. I did not replace that evidence with a timing-based correctness claim.

EXP-006 removes another 512/768/1024 keygen Barrett calls, reducing EXP-005's 1536/2304/3072 to 1024/1536/2048. Montgomery and encap/decap reduction counts remain unchanged. The dedicated dot function adds 285/355/413 bytes; combined with the changed keygen body the scoped net increase is 266/364/405 bytes. Kyber768 keygen unrolls a formerly looped sequence; its local stack changes from 9384 to 9368 bytes, with Kyber512/1024 unchanged at 5272/15032. These are scoped bodies/local frames, not complete executable size or peak KEM stack.

I additionally extracted four encap/decap functions from each timing binary: KEM enc_derand/dec and IND-CPA enc/dec. All twelve control/candidate body comparisons match after relocation normalisation. KEM entries match; IND-CPA entries shift by -16 bytes for Kyber512, +16 for Kyber768 and zero for Kyber1024. Other call targets/layout locations need not be identical. The observed control-operation regressions therefore receive no claimed arithmetic benefit from this keygen-only change. Layout, compiler decisions and environmental variation are possible contributors, not demonstrated causes. I do not claim to have measured instruction-cache effects or isolated the cost of one reduction.

Keygen canaries, endbr64 and stack-page probes remain as reviewed. The EXP-002 general-dot local-canary removal persists; the new dedicated dot likewise has no local canary. No protection flag was disabled. Code presence does not establish active CET enforcement, complete memory safety or physical side-channel resistance. Reviewed branches/address choices in the changed arithmetic depend on public indices/bounds, but this is scoped assembly evidence, not whole-program constant-time proof. Larger intermediate representatives also alter bit patterns; timing and functional evidence do not answer power/EM/fault questions.

## Closing evaluation and security transition

I carry EXP-005 forward as the primary candidate because EXP-006's benefit is not general and comes with code growth and measured regressions. EXP-006's Kyber1024 keygen result remains worth reporting and, if selection depends on it, confirming in fresh matched sessions. I make no new per-parameter dispatch or arithmetic rollback in this activity; faithful historical restoration supplies EXP-005 to the tests.

[EXP-007](../experiments/EXP-007-secret-flow.md) now specifies executable dynamic checking of original/EXP-005/EXP-006 for all parameters. The ACT-0016 development check completes nine real builds and 198 Memcheck processes. All eighteen deliberately marked positive controls are detected; all defined twins, marked negative controls and 72 marked target processes are quiet. All 102 extracted production bodies match accepted normal binaries after relocation normalisation; the 27 control bodies are also retained and checked. The evidence is explicitly a development support check with uncommitted support, not a formal EXP-007 run or security certificate. Formal collection follows the support commit. Statistical timing-leakage testing remains required and not-run. VTune remains excluded.

I generated [thesis figures and tables](../thesis_figures.md), including all paired effects and the adverse results. The next practical stages are formal secret-flow assessment, the statistical leakage pilot/protocol and runs, then direct original-reference comparisons with repeat sessions and the final reproducibility/security summary. I do not need another optimisation to replace a mixed result.
