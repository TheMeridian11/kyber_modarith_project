## Arithmetic and implementation security

The centred-input contract is essential: every current KEM inverse-transform input comes from the vector dot product's retained final poly_reduce, giving coefficients in [-1664,1664]. The reviewed bounds cover the current malformed/noncanonical input paths through the earlier EXP-002 argument. I retain the surrounding caller and downstream reductions.

The stage output bounds are 3328, 6656, 13312, 3328, 6656, 13312 and 26624. Every sum/difference stays within 26624 and is representable in int16_t. Twiddle products are bounded by 44302336 and final scaling products by 38365184, within int32_t and the Montgomery input interval [-109084672,109084671]. Final inverse outputs are bounded by 3328. Subsequent encryption/decryption intermediates remain within 3330/4995/6656 before their unchanged reductions. The unchanged twiddle order, Montgomery operations and factor 1441 preserve the required scale. Different internal representatives modulo q are permitted; external tested bytes remain equal.

These conclusions do not extend to arbitrary int16_t input arrays. Removing an input reduction, changing the stage-16 reset or widening the caller envelope requires a new range assessment. Passing UBSan does not alone establish narrowing safety: promoted int arithmetic and later int16_t conversions require the explicit argument above. Inherited implementation-defined signed narrowing and negative right-shift assumptions remain tied to the tested compiler/target.

I inspected invntt, montgomery_reduce and barrett_reduce in all six normal validation binaries, and extracted the corresponding functions from the three retained EXP-002 timing binaries after verifying their hashes. The original baseline and EXP-002 have matching normalised instructions for these three functions. Across parameters, each variant's reviewed instructions are identical after replacing the parameter namespace. Function byte coverage agrees with the symbol table.

GCC 13.3.0 moves the public length == 16 choice outside the butterfly body and generates two loop bodies. The stage-selection branch, group/butterfly termination branches and final scaling loop depend on public counters or fixed pointer endpoints. Zeta/input/output addresses use public indices and fixed offsets. No coefficient-dependent branch/address, division or indirect call was identified in these reviewed bodies. The code sometimes zero-extends coefficient loads and uses low 16-bit results before sign extension; the proved representability bounds make these operations consistent with the intended signed arithmetic.

EXP-003 has one static Barrett call site and three Montgomery call sites: general butterflies, length-16 butterflies and final scaling. These are not per-transform dynamic counts. The length-16 Barrett site executes 128 times and the Montgomery sites total 1152 calls. Both reducer bodies remain instruction-identical: Montgomery 22 bytes, Barrett 36 bytes, with fixed arithmetic and no data-dependent branch.
