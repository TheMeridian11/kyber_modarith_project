## Interpretation and security scope

Both cumulative variants beat the original in all 108 paired operation/block comparisons in this session. EXP-005's extra keygen benefit is positive at the session level for every parameter, while its Kyber1024 encapsulation and decapsulation effects are negative. I retain adverse pairs and use the complete three-session evidence to decide the primary candidate; this valid attempt is not replaced by a more favourable run.
