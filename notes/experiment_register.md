# Experiment register

The five arithmetic treatments were evaluated as cumulative changes to the pinned reference implementation. The closing comparison selected EXP-005 with explicit performance and implementation-security tradeoffs. EXP-003 remains a substantial comparator; EXP-006 remains a mixed/adverse finding. All planned collection and final-source review are complete.

| ID | Question or treatment | Lifecycle | Evidence and result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [EXP-000](experiments/EXP-000-baseline.md) | Establish a reproducible reference baseline and validation procedure | closed | RUN-0001 metadata, RUN-0002 functional checks, RUN-0003 timing pilot and [RUN-0004 counts](runs/EXP-000-RUN-0004.md) accepted | baseline preparation complete; security claims remain bounded by evidence |
| [EXP-001](experiments/EXP-001-fused-basemul.md) | Fuse products before Montgomery reduction within base multiplication | closed | [Validation](runs/EXP-001-RUN-0001.md) and [timings](runs/EXP-001-RUN-0002.md) accepted; approximately 4-6% improvement for Kyber512 and 7-9% for Kyber1024; Kyber768 keygen +1.68%, encap/decap inconclusive | keep as a validated research reference; no universal or complete security claim |
| [EXP-002](experiments/EXP-002-wide-dot.md) | Fuse reductions across a complete vector dot product | closed | [Validation](runs/EXP-002-RUN-0001.md) and [timings](runs/EXP-002-RUN-0002.md) accepted; all 45 pairs faster than EXP-001, median reductions 2.02-6.07% / 11.83-13.55% / 8.09-8.66% for Kyber512/768/1024 | keep as the next research reference with documented bounds, platform limits and local-canary tradeoff |
| [EXP-003](experiments/EXP-003-staged-invntt.md) | Reduce Barrett frequency in inverse NTT on top of EXP-002 | closed | [Validation](runs/EXP-003-RUN-0001.md) and [timings](runs/EXP-003-RUN-0002.md) accepted; all 30 encap/decap pairs improve, median reductions 4.30–4.98%; keygen medians -0.31%/-0.51%/+0.70% | keep for targeted benefit, with small observed keygen tradeoffs and inherited bounds/security limits |
| EXP-004 | Investigate a separate interaction or combination question if needed | proposed | Reserved idea; no implementation or run. Existing variants are already cumulative, so repeating that same combination would duplicate work | defer until separate variants/controlled ablations answer a distinct interaction question; not a failed or rejected experiment |
| [EXP-005](experiments/EXP-005-error-ntt.md) | Defer only keygen error-vector post-NTT reduction on top of EXP-003 | closed | [Validation](runs/EXP-005-RUN-0001.md) and [timings](runs/EXP-005-RUN-0002.md) accepted; keygen +5.18%/+0.48%/+1.36%, all fifteen keygen pairs positive; Kyber1024 encap/decap -0.12%/-0.50%; keygen canaries retained, code/local-stack tradeoffs recorded | supported for observed keygen benefit; keep as next research reference, with final primary selection accepted in ACT-0020 after repeat-session/security review |
| [EXP-006](experiments/EXP-006-keygen-dot.md) | Defer keygen dot-output centring before retained scale conversion | closed | [Correctness/assembly](runs/EXP-006-RUN-0001.md) and [timing review](runs/EXP-006-RUN-0002.md) accepted; keygen -2.55%/+0.22%/+1.67%, code growth and control-operation regressions | general-benefit hypothesis not supported; preserve mixed/parameter-specific result; EXP-005 is primary closing candidate |
| [EXP-007](experiments/EXP-007-secret-flow.md) | Assess dynamic secret flow in original/EXP-005/EXP-006 | closed | [RUN-0001](runs/EXP-007-RUN-0001.md) accepted: 144 quiet target processes, 18 expected positive detections, quiet defined/negative controls; 129 matching assembly bodies | no dependency observed in exercised paths; no whole-KEM or instruction-latency claim |
| [EXP-008](experiments/EXP-008-timing-leakage.md) | Screen secret-class timing in original/EXP-003/EXP-005 | closed | [RUN-0001](runs/EXP-008-RUN-0001.md) accepted: 288 quiet target processes, maximum absolute t 2.91016 below 10, all 36 controls meet criteria; 1944 statistics reproduced | no difference detected within budget; retain finite-path/class/platform limits |
| [EXP-009](experiments/EXP-009-final-comparison.md) | Direct original/EXP-003/EXP-005 comparison across sessions | closed | [All three sessions](final_comparison.md) accepted: 972000 duration rows; identical frozen support/source/tool conditions; every original-reference pair improves | select [EXP-005](final_candidate.md) for repeated additional keygen benefit; retain Kyber1024 control-operation regressions and EXP-003 as alternative |

K is 2, 3 or 4. EXP-002 includes EXP-001, EXP-003 includes EXP-002 and EXP-005 includes EXP-003. Their incremental effects are conditional on the preceding implementation, not independent main effects. Separate-run median gains are not added or multiplied to obtain the total improvement over the original.

EXP-004 was reserved for a separate interaction/ablation question. Existing cumulative variants do not isolate interactions, so that experiment was deferred without implementation or measurement. It is not a failed trial. The [final comparison](final_comparison.md), [selection rationale](final_candidate.md) and [security conclusion](final_security_summary.md) give the completed project-wide interpretation.
